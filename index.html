Who we serve

Owners whose numbers stopped keeping up

Construction is our home ground — WIP schedules, retainage, and bonding capacity are native language here. The same clarity discipline serves any owner-led business where operations outgrew the bookkeeping.

Construction owners & specialty contractors

Profitable on paper, cash-tight in practice. Margin known only at job's end. Bonding capacity smaller than ambition. Sound familiar — in the specific, WIP-and-retainage sense?

Entrepreneurs starting & scaling

First entity, first crew, first bid that has to carry real overhead. Build gets the economics right while mistakes are still cheap.

Middle-market executives

You need a forecast you can defend to a bank, a surety, or your own board — and reporting that arrives while decisions are still open.

Why 1LINE is different

We sell the cadence, not the report

Reports describe the past. A cadence changes the future: the same short review, on the same day, ending in priced, owned actions. Four commitments make that real.

More about how 1LINE works →

  • A named CFO, not a diffuse team

    Every engagement is led by founder Jake Schreiber — a practicing CFO who sits in the chair you're trying to fill. Judgment is signed, not crowdsourced.

  • One version of the truth

    Definitions documented once, in a KPI dictionary. If two reports disagree, that's treated as a defect — not a meeting topic.

  • Fit / no-fit honesty

    If your real gap is bookkeeping, we'll say so and point you there. Selling strategy on top of unreliable numbers helps no one.

  • Free value first

    Academy's guides and tools are free because trust is earned by being useful before anyone signs anything.
