Divisions overview

Five divisions. One line.

Each division solves a different stage of the same problem: turning financial information into decisions. They share one founder, one set of definitions, and one standard — if two 1LINE reports ever disagree, that's a defect, not a discussion.

01

1LINE Advisory

Fractional CFO leadership, on a clear monthly cadence

Advisory is 1LINE's fractional CFO practice: financial strategy, forecasting, management reporting, and decision support for owners who need executive-grade finance without hiring a full-time CFO.

Start here if: Construction company owners and specialty contractors whose reporting no longer keeps up with the business

Explore →
02

1LINE Build

Launch, operate, and scale a contractor business — on sound economics

Build helps skilled tradespeople becoming owners — and established contractors fixing their foundations — put the financial and operating systems in place before growth exposes the gaps.

Start here if: Licensed tradespeople starting their first construction company

Explore →
03

1LINE Intelligence

Dashboards, reporting, and automation that turn data into decisions

Intelligence turns scattered operational and financial data — job cost, WIP, receivables, backlog — into a small set of maintained dashboards and exception reports that owners actually read.

Start here if: Companies with real systems (accounting, project management, spreadsheets) but no single operating view

Explore →
04

1LINE Academy

Practical business and finance education — free first, useful always

Academy is 1LINE's education division: free articles, guides, templates, and practical explainers on construction finance and business leadership — built to be useful before anything is ever sold.

Start here if: Contractors and business owners who want to read their own financials with confidence

Explore →
05

1LINE Capital

Financing readiness, bonding readiness, and enterprise value

Capital prepares owners for the moments when outsiders judge the business: a credit line, a bonding increase, a growth investment, or an eventual exit. Preparation and strategy — presented honestly as advisory, not as lending or banking.

Start here if: Owners planning growth that will require bank or surety support
