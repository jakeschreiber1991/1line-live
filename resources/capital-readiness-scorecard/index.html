Capital Readiness Scorecard

Lenders and sureties score your business whether you see the scorecard or not. This one is yours: answer honestly, one point for each statement that is fully true today. The lowest section is where preparation starts.

Financial reporting (score /5)

  • ✓Monthly financial statements are closed within 15 days of month-end.
  • ✓The WIP schedule reconciles to the income statement every month.
  • ✓Statements are prepared or reviewed by a CPA at least annually.
  • ✓You can produce a current backlog report with margins by job on request.
  • ✓Interim statements match year-end presentation — no surprises in format or policy.

Working capital (score /5)

  • ✓You know your retainage receivable balance to the dollar, today.
  • ✓Receivables over 60 days are a small, known, actively worked list.
  • ✓A 13-week cash forecast exists and is updated weekly.
  • ✓Underbilling is reviewed monthly with named reasons.
  • ✓The credit line is drawn deliberately, not discovered to be drawn.

Banking & bonding (score /5)

  • ✓You know your current single-job and aggregate bonding limits — and what drives them.
  • ✓Covenant calculations (if any) are computed before the bank asks.
  • ✓Your banker and surety have seen your forecast, not just your history.
  • ✓Owner compensation and related-party items are documented and explainable.
  • ✓You could assemble a complete lender package within five business days.

Scoring guide: 12–15 — ready; preparation is maintenance. 8–11 — fundable with fixable gaps; close the lowest section first. Below 8 — the gaps are the plan, and they are cheaper to close before a lender finds them. 1LINE Capital prepares owners section by section; see the Capital page for how that works. This scorecard is education, not a lending decision or a guarantee of any outcome.
