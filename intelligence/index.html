The problems

What we do

Core services

01

Management dashboards

One executive view — cash, WIP, margin, backlog, receivables — defined once, refreshed on a cadence, identical for every reader.

02

Automated reporting workflows

The monthly pack assembled by process instead of by heroics: imports checked, variances flagged, definitions documented.

03

Forecasting models

Driver-based models for cash, backlog burn, and capacity that update from actuals instead of being rebuilt.

04

Business intelligence foundations

A KPI dictionary and data-quality checks, so every number on the dashboard has one agreed meaning and a traceable source.

05

Practical AI assistance

AI used where it is genuinely useful — exception triage, variance drafting, research assistance — always with human approval before anything is sent, filed, or booked.

Outcomes

What changes

  • ✓One version of the numbers, available before the meeting instead of after it
  • ✓Exceptions surfaced weekly, while they are still small
  • ✓Month-end reporting time cut by process, not by working later
