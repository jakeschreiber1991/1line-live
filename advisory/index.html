Who it's for

Built for owners at a specific moment

  • →Construction company owners and specialty contractors whose reporting no longer keeps up with the business
  • →Middle-market executives who need a forecast they can defend — to a bank, a surety, or their own team
  • →Owners making consequential calls on hiring, equipment, backlog, and pricing with incomplete numbers

The problems

What brings people to this page

  • ✕Profitable on paper, short on cash — and no one can explain the gap
  • ✕Month-end reports arrive too late to change anything
  • ✕Job margins are known only after the job is finished
  • ✕Growth decisions are made on instinct because the forecast is a guess

What we do

Core services

01

Financial planning & forecasting

Rolling forecasts tied to backlog, capacity, and cash — updated on a cadence, not rebuilt from scratch each quarter.

02

Cash flow management

A maintained 13-week cash view that surfaces shortfalls while there is still time to act on them.

03

KPI & management reporting

A short, consistent reporting pack — WIP, margin by job, backlog quality, receivables — written for decisions, not for filing.

04

Profitability analysis

Margin bridges that show where profit is made and lost: estimating, labor, change orders, overhead absorption.

05

Strategic advisory

A standing financial partner for pricing, hiring, capital purchases, and growth decisions — with the numbers worked before the meeting.

Outcomes

What changes

  • ✓A forecast the owner, the bank, and the surety can all read the same way
  • ✓Cash surprises replaced by a 13-week line of sight
  • ✓Margin problems identified while the job can still be corrected
  • ✓Decisions documented with their financial reasoning, not reconstructed later
