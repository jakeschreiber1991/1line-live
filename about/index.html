About 1LINE

One clear line from information to decision

1LINE (1Line LLC, founded 2024 in Southern California) is a financial leadership and business advisory firm. The name comes from the electrical one-line diagram: an entire system — source, path, and load — drawn so it can be understood at a glance. We believe a business's finances deserve the same discipline.

The problem we exist to solve is not a lack of data. Modern businesses drown in reports. The problem is translation: job-cost systems, accounting ledgers, bank balances, and backlog spreadsheets each tell a partial truth, in a different format, on a different schedule — and the owner is left to assemble the decision alone.

Our answer is structural. One set of documented definitions. One maintained view of cash, margin, and backlog. One recurring cadence that ends in decisions with owners, prices, and dates. Five divisions carry that discipline to the five places owners need it: leadership, foundations, data, education, and capital readiness.

Construction is our home ground — our founder practices as a CFO in the industry, where percent-complete accounting, retainage, and bonding make financial clarity a survival skill rather than a nicety. The parent brand is deliberately broader: any owner-led business whose operations have outgrown its bookkeeping benefits from the same line.

Jake Schreiber

Jake Schreiber is a practicing chief financial officer in the construction industry and a UCLA MBA (2025). He founded 1LINE to give owner-led businesses — contractors first — the financial clarity he builds inside companies every day: one clear line from the numbers to the next decision.

How we work

Four operating principles

Clarity over volume

A short report that changes a decision beats a thick one that decorates a shelf. Everything we produce has a decision attached, or it stops being produced.

Honesty about scope

We describe capabilities as they are. Divisions in development are labeled as such; we never present planned services, invented results, or testimonials we don't have.

Construction depth, business breadth

Our methods were forged in the hardest accounting environment in business. We apply them wherever owner-led operations have outgrown their financial visibility.

Free value first

Academy's library is free and stays free. A firm that teaches clearly, in public, earns the right to be hired privately.

Ready to draw your line?

Tell us where the numbers stop making sense. The first conversation is a working session, not a sales pitch — you keep the findings either way.
