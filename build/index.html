What we do

Core services

01

Startup planning

Entity, licensing, and compliance sequencing for California contractors, with the financial setup done in the right order — and referrals to licensed professionals where the work requires them.

02

Financial controls

A chart of accounts built for job costing, a weekly cash habit, and simple controls that survive a busy season.

03

Estimating & business economics

Bid math that carries labor burden, overhead, and target margin — so the price is a decision, not a hope.

04

Operating processes

Practical systems for billing, collections, change orders, and close-out that a small team can actually run.

05

Scaling readiness

An honest assessment of what breaks next — people, cash, bonding, or systems — before you commit to bigger work.
